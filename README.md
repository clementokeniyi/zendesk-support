# Zendesk Support Portfolio — CJays Solutions

A fully configured Zendesk Support environment built to demonstrate 
real-world customer support administration skills.

**Environment:** cjayssolutions.zendesk.com (Trial)  
**Built by:** Clement Okeniyi

---

## What I Built and Why

### 1. Custom Ticket Views
Created three custom views to organize agent workflow:
- **Open Tickets** — gives agents a focused list of all active tickets
- **Pending Tickets** — surfaces tickets waiting on customer response so nothing falls through the cracks
- **Solved Tickets (Last 30 Days)** — allows team leads to review recently closed tickets for quality checks

> Views reduce the time agents spend searching for tickets and ensure 
> priority work is always visible.

---

### 2. Macros (Canned Responses)
Built three macros for the most common agent interactions:
- **Acknowledge - Ticket Received** — sets the right tone from first contact
- **Follow Up - Waiting on Customer** — keeps tickets moving without manual effort
- **Resolve - Issue Fixed** — closes the ticket and confirms resolution in one action

> Macros reduce average handle time and ensure consistent, professional 
> communication across the team regardless of which agent responds.

---

### 3. Triggers (Automation Rules)
Configured two triggers to automate ticket handling:
- **Auto-Reply on Ticket Creation** — customer receives an instant confirmation with their ticket ID, reducing "did you get my email?" follow-ups
- **Auto-Assign to Support Group** — new tickets are automatically routed to the support group, eliminating manual assignment

> Triggers remove repetitive manual tasks from the agent workflow and 
> improve the customer experience through faster response times.

---

### 4. SLA Policy
Created a Standard Support SLA with tiered response targets by priority:
- Urgent: 1 hour first reply
- High: 4 hours first reply
- Normal: 8 hours first reply
- Low: 24 hours first reply

> SLA policies create accountability and give customers predictable 
> response expectations. They also allow managers to measure team 
> performance against defined standards.

---

### 5. Help Center (Knowledge Base)
Built a structured Help Center with:
- **Category:** Getting Started
- **Section:** Common Questions
- **3 Published Articles:**
  - How to Submit a Support Ticket
  - How to Reset Your Password
  - How to Check the Status of Your Ticket

> A well-maintained knowledge base deflects repetitive tickets, reduces 
> agent workload, and empowers customers to self-serve — a key metric 
> in modern support operations.

---

### 6. Custom Ticket Field — Issue Category
Added a dropdown field with the following values:
- Billing
- Technical Issue
- Account Access
- General Inquiry
- Feature Request

> Custom fields enable ticket tagging and reporting. By categorizing 
> every ticket, support managers can identify the most common issue 
> types, spot trends, and make data-driven decisions about staffing, 
> training, and product improvements.
